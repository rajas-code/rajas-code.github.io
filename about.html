02 / About

The person behind
the questions.

Hi, I’m Rajapandi Nadar. I’m a physics researcher and teacher from Mumbai, fascinated by the universe’s most extreme phenomena.

My work often revolves around pulsars, X-ray binaries, and the occasional gravitational-wave puzzle: cosmic clocks and fireworks that keep astrophysics exciting.

I have carried out research projects at TIFR and St. Xavier’s College, exploring pulsar timing, binary evolution, and high-energy telescope data.

Alongside research, I conduct lectures in physics for college and high-school students, sharing concepts in ways that make science approachable and engaging.

Questions are where both research and good teaching begin.

Researcher, educator, lifelong learner
