Hi, I’m Rajapandi Nadar. I’m a physics researcher and teacher from Mumbai, fascinated by cosmic clocks, stellar explosions, and the stories data can tell when we ask better questions.

I have worked on pulsar timing, binary evolution, and high-energy telescope data at TIFR and St. Xavier’s College. Alongside research, I conduct physics lectures for college and high-school students.
